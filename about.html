How It Started

A Belief in the Power of Place

Nook and Haven was born from a simple belief: the places we inhabit—even briefly—have the power to change us. Whether it's a weekend retreat that reignites your creativity, a vacation home where your family makes memories, or finding that perfect house that finally feels like home, place matters.

We started this because we've experienced it ourselves—that moment when you walk into a space and just know. The light hits differently. The air feels clearer. Something clicks. We wanted to create a platform that helps people find those moments, whether they last a weekend, a season, or a lifetime.

We're not just listing properties or booking stays. We're curators of possibility, believers in the transformative power of place, and your partners in finding where you belong.
