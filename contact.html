Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a vacation stay?

Browse our Stays page, find a property you love, and click "Check Availability" or "View Details." You can inquire directly through the listing or contact us and we'll help you find the perfect match.

How do I list my property on Nook & Haven?

We'd love to hear about your space! Fill out the contact form above or email properties@nookandhaven.co. We review every property personally to ensure it meets our standards.

What's included in a retreat?

Each retreat is unique, but most include accommodations, meals, guided sessions, materials, and free time for exploration. Check individual retreat pages for full details on what's included.

How does the home buying process work with The Prince Team?

Reach out and we'll schedule a conversation to understand what you're looking for. From there, we'll curate showings, guide you through offers and negotiations, and be with you every step to closing day.
